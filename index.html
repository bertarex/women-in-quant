About M2M

Mathematics to Markets is a new student-led initiative for students studying Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science and other STEM subjects who are interested in where analytical thinking can take them beyond university.

We want to show that STEM degrees are valuable far beyond academia — in trading, finance, consulting, technology, entrepreneurship and the everyday decisions behind successful businesses.

What We Want To Build

M2M is starting as a space for curious students to explore the connection between mathematics, markets, business and real-world problem solving.

STEM to Careers

Helping students understand how maths, physics, computer science and related degrees can lead into finance, trading, consulting, tech and business.

Mathematics in the Real World

Exploring how mathematical thinking appears in pricing, strategy, operations, markets, queues, restaurants, sport and decision-making.

Community

Creating a place for students interested in quantitative and analytical careers to meet, ask questions and learn from each other.

Stories & Conversations

Speaking with students, alumni, professionals and local businesses about how ideas grow into real opportunities.

Coming Up

Ideas We’re Exploring

As M2M develops, we want to create content and events that make the link between mathematics and the real world more accessible, practical and interesting.

  • Introductory events on finance, trading, consulting and technology careers
  • Short interviews with alumni, founders, professionals and local businesses
  • Random facts connecting mathematics to markets, pricing, restaurants, queues, strategy and everyday decisions
  • Conversations about how STEM students can use their degrees in unexpected career paths
  • Future podcast-style episodes featuring people with interesting stories from St Andrews and beyond

For example, we would love to explore stories from local businesses: how they started, how they grew, what decisions mattered, and where maths, strategy or problem-solving appears behind the scenes.
