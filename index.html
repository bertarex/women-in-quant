About Us

Women in Quant is a student initiative created by mathematics students who want to explore the transition from mathematics into real-world careers such as finance, quantitative trading, data science, consulting and technology.

Our Mission

We aim to bridge the gap between university mathematics and industry careers by providing:

  • Insight into quantitative finance and trading
  • Career guidance for consulting and tech roles
  • Networking with professionals and alumni
  • Support and visibility for women in quantitative fields

Podcast

We are launching a podcast featuring conversations with:

  • Quants and traders
  • Data scientists and machine learning engineers
  • Consultants and finance professionals
  • Students transitioning from mathematics into industry

The goal is to make career pathways more accessible and less intimidating by sharing real stories.
