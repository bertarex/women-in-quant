About M2M

Mathematics to Markets connects students interested in quantitative and analytical careers with the skills, people and opportunities needed to move from academic mathematics into industry.

Our Mission

We aim to make career paths in finance, trading, consulting, data science and technology more accessible to mathematically minded students.

Mathematics to Industry

Helping students understand how mathematical skills translate into real-world roles.

Community

Building a network of students interested in quantitative and analytical careers.

Career Exposure

Hosting speakers, alumni and professionals from finance, tech and consulting.

Practical Skills

Workshops in Python, modelling, data analysis and interview preparation.

Podcast

The M2M Podcast explores how students and professionals move from mathematics into industry.

  • Breaking into quantitative finance
  • Careers in trading, risk and asset management
  • Data science and technology roles
  • Consulting and analytical career paths

Real conversations, practical advice and honest insights from people navigating quantitative careers.

Events
