About M2M

Mathematics to Markets connects students interested in quantitative and analytical careers with the skills, people and opportunities needed to move from academic mathematics into industry.

Our Mission

We aim to make career paths in finance, trading, consulting, data science and technology more accessible to mathematically minded students.
