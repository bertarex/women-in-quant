About M2M

Mathematics to Markets is a new student-led project at St Andrews.

It started from something we noticed: a lot of students in maths, physics, computer science and other STEM subjects are interested in finance, trading, consulting, technology or business, but do not always know where to start.

We want M2M to be a place to explore those routes in a simple, honest and interesting way.

What We Do

The idea is not just to talk about careers, but to look at where mathematical thinking shows up in real life.

STEM to Careers

Introductory events and conversations about finance, trading, consulting, technology and business.

Maths in Real Life

Short ideas and examples connecting maths to pricing, decisions, queues, markets, restaurants, sport and strategy.

People & Stories

Interviews with students, alumni, professionals and local businesses about how they got started.

Community

A place for students with similar interests to meet, ask questions and learn together.

Stories

We want to speak to people with interesting stories — from alumni working in finance or consulting to local businesses in St Andrews.

  • How did they start?
  • What decisions helped them grow?
  • Where do maths, strategy or problem-solving appear behind the scenes?
  • What can students learn from them?

This could become short interviews, written posts or podcast-style conversations as M2M grows.
